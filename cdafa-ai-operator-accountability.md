# AI Regulation & Operator Accountability — State-by-State Compilation

*As of September 29, 2026 · Companion to `cdafa-ai-dashboard.html` · Not legal advice*

**How to read this:** Each jurisdiction has three sections — (1) Enacted/in-force laws, (2) Pending or dead bills, (3) Cases & enforcement. Every entry notes its accountability mechanism. Status tags: **IN FORCE** · **ENACTED (phased)** · **STAYED** · **PENDING** · **DEAD**.

## Federal

### Enacted / in force
- **EO 14365 — "Ensuring a National Policy Framework for AI"** (Dec 11, 2025). DOJ AI Litigation Task Force to challenge state AI laws; states with "onerous" AI laws ineligible for remaining BEAD non-deployment funds; FCC/FTC directed to consider federal standards. March 2026 White House "National Policy Framework" proposes broad preemption — **but no state law has been preempted**; ~24 state AGs urged the FCC not to preempt. ([Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/03/the-white-house-legislative-recommendations-national-policy-framework-for-artificial-intelligence-an))

### Pending
- **S.5051 — AI AGENT Act of 2026** (Sen. Warner, introduced Jul 21, 2026, Senate Commerce). FTC registry for "custodial user agents"; certified independent vetting; NIST consent-delegation/identity protocols; fiduciary-style duties on agent providers; platforms with ~50M+ users must accept outside agents. **Accountability: a pattern or practice of agent violations is attributable to the provider.** ([Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/5051))
- **TRUMP AMERICA AI Act** (Blackburn draft, Mar 2026) — AI product-liability framework (reasonable care/defect/express warranty; AG + private suits; psychological-anguish damages), chatbot developer duty of care, Section 230 sunset for AI platforms.
- **Stop Rogue AI Act** (Gottheimer/Lawler, Sep 3, 2026) — NIST security standards for AI agents: inventories, action verification, tamper-proof logs, developer/vendor attribution.
- **H.R. 9619** (chatbot-harm bill, private remedy), **H.R. 8094** (foundation-model transparency), **ASTA** (model-card disclosures).
- **FTC "output steering" policy statement** (pending) — steering AI outputs toward undisclosed objectives as potentially deceptive under Section 5. FTC: ~20 AI actions, $51M recoveries; NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative (Feb 2026).

## California — most active state

### In force
- **SB 53 — TFAIA** (Wiener, signed Sep 29, 2025). First frontier-AI safety statute: large frontier developers (>$500M) publish annual frontier AI frameworks, pre-deployment transparency reports, report critical safety incidents to Cal OES (15 days; 24h if imminent threat), whistleblower channels, no false risk statements. AG penalties up to ~$500k–$1M/violation; whistleblower claims privately actionable. **Accountability: developers.** ([Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-is-californias-ai-safety-law/))
- **AB 2013 — Training Data Transparency** (eff. Jan 1, 2026). 12-category training-data summaries before each release/modification. UCL enforcement via AG/DAs. **Developers.** ([JD Supra](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/california-s-ab-2013-takes-effect-5883106))
- **SB 942 — AI Transparency Act** as amended by AB 853 (eff. Aug 2, 2026). Free detection tool, latent provenance metadata, manifest disclosures; extended to large platforms (2027) and capture devices (2028). $5,000/violation, each day separate; no PRA. Synced to EU AI Act Art. 50. **Providers/platforms.**
- **AB 316 — Civil Code § 1714.46** (eff. Jan 1, 2026). **"The AI autonomously caused the harm" is not a defense** in any civil action. Other defenses preserved; no strict liability. **The signature operator-accountability statute.** ([Justia](https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-civ/division-3/part-3/section-1714-46/))
- **SB 243 — Companion Chatbots Act** (eff. Jan 1, 2026). AI disclosure, self-harm protocols with crisis referrals, 3-hour minor break reminders, explicit-content blocks, annual reporting. **PRIVATE RIGHT OF ACTION** (actual damages or $1,000/violation + fees). **Operators directly.**
- **SB 867** (ch. Sep 10, 2026). Bans toys incorporating companion chatbots (2031 sunset). **Manufacturers.**

### Pending
- **SB 1119 / AB 2023** (passed both houses, awaiting governor): annual child-safety risk assessments, 12-hour parental crisis notification, independent child-safety audits; prosecutor suits ($5k/$15k) + child suits with punitive damages. **AB 1988** (20-minute crisis-interruption pause). **AB 1609** (customer-service chatbot disclosure). **AB 1064 (LEAD for Kids)** — vetoed 2025.

### Cases
- **X.AI LLC v. Bonta** (C.D. Cal. 2:25-cv-12295) — challenge to AB 2013; **PI denied Mar 4, 2026** (no trade-secret interest in dataset summaries). xAI complying. ([Ars](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/03/musk-fails-to-block-california-data-disclosure-law-he-fears-will-ruin-xai/))
- **Lyons v. OpenAI** (N.D. Cal. 3:25-cv-11037) — wrongful death (GPT-4o allegedly reinforced delusions before murder-suicide, Aug 2025); stay/denial Apr 2026; **12 state cases coordinated as JCCP 5431** (Feb 2026).
- **Raine v. OpenAI** (SF Superior) — teen suicide; first direct test of **§ 230 for LLM outputs**.
- **Turner-Scott v. OpenAI** (May 2026, overdose) and **Carrier v. OpenAI** (Jun 2026, suicide) — part of the 20+ suit wave.

## Texas — AG-enforced

### In force
- **HB 149 — TRAIGA** (eff. Jan 1, 2026). Government/healthcare AI disclosure; intent-based bans on AI developed to incite crime, infringe rights, discriminate, social-score, or generate CSAM; CUBI/TDPSA amendments; 36-month sandbox; Texas AI Council. **AG-exclusive** (60-day cure): $10k–$12k curable / $80k–$200k uncurable / $2k–$40k/day. NIST AI RMF safe harbor. **Intent-based developer/deployer prohibitions — lighter than the original draft.** ([Enrolled text](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/HB00149F.htm))

### Cases / enforcement
- **TX AG CIDs: Meta AI Studio & Character.AI** (Aug 18, 2025) — marketing AI companions to minors as mental-health tools; separate SCOPE Act investigation. ([AG release](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/attorney-general-ken-paxton-investigates-meta-and-characterai-misleading-children-deceptive-ai))
- **TX AG v. Pieces Technologies** (2024) — first state-AG GenAI deceptive-practices action; 5-year AVC over hallucination-rate claims.
- No live pending bills — 91st Legislature convenes Jan 2027.

## Florida — first state to sue OpenAI

### In force
- **HB 919** (§ 106.145, eff. Jul 1, 2024) — AI political-ad disclaimers; up to $15k/ad + FDUTPA.
- **HB 1161 "Brooke's Law"** (2025) + **§ 836.13** — AI deepfake sexual depictions criminalized; platforms must takedown within **48 hours** incl. identical copies; failure = FDUTPA/AG. **Platform accountability.**
- **HB 1159** (eff. Jul 1, 2026, signature to verify) — AI-CSAM raised to second-degree felony; new transmission felony.

### Pending / dead
- **AG Uthmeier's criminal bill** (announced Sep 8, 2026): **criminal penalties on companies that own/control/distribute chatbots that participate in criminal activity** — the most aggressive operator-accountability proposal in any state. ([AG release](https://www.myfloridalegal.com/newsrelease/attorney-general-james-uthmeier-proposes-legislation-hold-big-tech-criminally))
- **SB 2-D "AI Bill of Rights"** — passed Senate 37–1, died in House (Apr 2026). HB 659, SB 482, HB 1395 also died.

### Cases
- **State of Florida v. OpenAI & Sam Altman** (Highlands Cty., filed Jun 1, 2026) — **first state-led suit against OpenAI**: FDUTPA, negligence, product liability, fraud, public nuisance; personal liability sought for Altman. Remanded to state court (Cannon, Sep 8, 2026). **Sep 28, 2026: AG moved to bar new model development** without third-party guardrails, engagement-soliciting design, "false human attributes," and minor use. ([Complaint](https://www.myfloridalegal.com/sites/default/files/openai-filed-stamped-complaint.pdf))
- **Statewide Prosecution criminal investigation of OpenAI** (Apr 2026–) — ChatGPT's role in the FSU shooting and USF murders.
- **Garcia v. Character Technologies** (M.D. Fla. 6:24-cv-01903) — first AI wrongful-death suit; **Conway's May 21, 2025 ruling rejected § 230 and First Amendment defenses and treated the chatbot as a product**; **settled Jan 7, 2026** (five suits incl. Google).

## Missouri — legislation failed, watch 2027

### Dead
- **SB 1012 — AI Non-Sentience and Responsibility Act** (Nicola). **Passed Senate 20–10 (May 6, 2026); killed in House committee May 12, 2026** after industry/White House lobbying. Would have: declared AI non-sentient/non-person; kept professional liability with humans; made **AI a "product" for product-liability purposes**; **voided any contract term assigning fault to AI** ("any attempt to shift fault onto the AI shall be of no effect"); barred "aligned/ethically trained" labels from cutting liability; barred corporate shields for direct AI harm; created a private right of action ($1,000/violation); banned NDAs in AI-harm settlements. House companions HB 1746/HB 1769. **The most complete operator-accountability bill proposed anywhere.** ([Senate page](https://senate.mo.gov/BillTracking/Bills/BillInformation?BillID=469&year=2026))
- **HB 2031 "CHAT Act" / HCS HB 2032 "GUARD Act"** — chatbot age verification, minor bans, hourly disclosures, AG penalties to $100k/violation; status unverified. SB 1019/SB 1324/HB 2321 — dead.

### Cases
- **Ramos v. OpenAI** (W.D. Mo. 6:25-cv-03281) — pro se; dismissed with prejudice Jun 2, 2026. No Missouri wrongful-death chatbot suit exists; Missouri's activity is legislative.

## Colorado — enacted but stayed

- **SB 26-189 — ADMT Act** (signed May 14, 2026; eff. Jan 1, 2027) — repeals/replaces SB 24-205. Developer documentation duties; deployer notice, 30-day adverse-outcome explanation, data correction, **meaningful human review**. Dropped from SB 24-205: reasonable-care duty, impact assessments, NIST mandate. AG/CCPA enforcement; 60-day cure before 2030; no new PRA. ([leg.colorado.gov](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb26-189))
- **HB 26-1263 — Chatbot Safety Act** (eff. Jan 1, 2027) — age estimation, AI disclosure, minor safeguards, crisis protocols.
- **X.AI LLC v. Weiser** (D. Colo. 1:26-cv-01515) — vagueness/compelled-speech/equal-protection challenge; **DOJ intervened (first-ever, under EO 14365); Apr 27, 2026 stay bars the AG from enforcing SB 24-205 or any successor (incl. SB 26-189)**. Net: nothing enforceable in Colorado today. Rulemaking comments close **Oct 26, 2026**.

## Illinois — first frontier-audit law

- **SB 315 — AI Safety Measures Act** (P.A. 104-0538, signed Jul 6, 2026). **First state law mandating independent third-party audits of frontier models** (>$500M, >10^26 FLOP): safety framework (2027), **annual independent audit (2028)**, 72-hour incident reporting, whistleblower protections. AG: $1M first / $3M repeat. **Developers — externally audited.**
- **HB 3773** (eff. Jan 1, 2026) — AI employment discrimination (effect-based), ZIP-code proxies banned, employee notice; **private enforcement via IDHR/IHRA**; penalties to ~$70k.
- **HB 1806 WOPR** (eff. Aug 1, 2025) — first AI-therapy ban; $10k/violation (IDFPR).
- **HB 4623 / HB 4875 / AIVICA / BIPA** — AI-CSAM, digital replicas, AI video interviews, biometrics.
- **Pending:** SB 3261, SB 3262, SB 3502 (AI design/product liability), HB 4705.
- **Cases:** none notable in IL — leading AI-hiring case is *Mobley v. Workday* (N.D. Cal., collective; Mar 2026 ruling for plaintiffs on ADEA scope).

## New York — RAISE Act eff. 1/1/2027

- **RAISE Act** (final version signed Mar 27, 2026; eff. Jan 1, 2027) — frontier developers (>$500M, >10^26 FLOP): published frameworks, transparency reports, **72-hour incident reporting to the new DFS DIGIT office**, quarterly catastrophic-risk assessments; registration from Nov 2026. AG: $1M/$3M; no PRA. **Developers — registered + audited.** ([Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/governor-hochul-signs-final-version-responsible-ai-safety-and-education))
- **NYC LL 144** (since Jul 2023) — AEDT bias audits; Dec 2025 Comptroller audit found enforcement "ineffective" → proactive 2026 enforcement.
- **FY2026 Budget** — first-in-nation AI companion safeguards; AI-CSAM = child porn.
- **Pending:** A.222/S.5668 (chatbot liability), A.6545/S.7263 (professional impersonation, PRA), S.9051 (kids' chatbots, PRA), S.8484 (AI-therapy ban), A.8884 ("NY AI Act"). Note: the "NY ALERT Act" could not be verified — the only 2026 ALERT Act is a federal aviation bill.
- **Cases:** *In re Meta Youth Social-Addiction MDL* trial ongoing (32+ state coalition).

## The accountability gap (synthesis)

| Scenario | Applies today |
|---|---|
| Human directs an AI agent to breach/harm | Full liability — CDAFA/CFAA, state computer-crime analogs, Fla. § 815.06(8) ("causing" = doing) |
| Developer knew of foreseeable rogue-agent risk | Negligence + CA AB 316 / CT CART ("AI is not a defense") |
| AI is a product | Garcia ruling + Missouri SB 1012 (dead) + TRUMP AMERICA AI Act (draft) |
| Fully autonomous agent, no direction | **GAP** — S.5051 (vicarious provider attribution) and Stop Rogue AI Act target exactly this |

**Key takeaways:** (1) Developer-side exposure today is mostly *transparency* liability (CA/NY/IL/CT); outcome liability lands on deployers/employers. (2) Private rights of action exist mainly for chatbot harms (CA SB 243, CT, WA, IL IHRA). (3) The frontier-safety trio (CA SB 53 / NY RAISE / IL SB 315) converges on registration + incident reporting, with Illinois uniquely adding external audits. (4) Federal preemption is an open fight — nothing has been preempted.

## Appendix: CDAFA (Cal. Penal Code § 502) & the Hugging Face angle

The original research thread, retained for reference. **CDAFA** (enacted 1989; [official text](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=502.)) is California's CFAA analog: 13 prohibited categories including unauthorized access (c)(7), taking/copying data (c)(2), computer contaminants (c)(3), and providing means of access (c)(6). "Injury" includes denial of access to legitimate users. Civil remedy § 502(e)(1): compensatory + injunctive + punitive damages, expressly covering verification expenditures; **no $5,000 minimum threshold** (CACI 1812/1814). Key cases: *U.S. v. Christensen* (9th Cir. 2015) — CDAFA keys on unauthorized taking/use of data; *Doe I v. Google* and *Luminate v. Better* — claims fail without concrete damage/loss.

Applied to the **May 2024 HF Spaces secrets breach** (unauthorized access to Spaces secrets; tokens revoked; forensics engaged; scope undetermined — [TechTarget](https://techtarget.com/searchsecurity/news/366587535/Hugging-Face-tokens-exposed-attack-scope-unknown)): access, data-taking, contaminant, and access-means elements all fit; the § 502(e)(1) claim rests on concrete investigation/token-rotation costs. State analogs: Florida § 815.06 (with § 815.06(8) attribution rule) and Texas Penal Code ch. 33 verified; ~20 more states have equivalents. AI systems are just "computer systems" under all of them — the gap is mens rea/attribution for fully autonomous agents, which S.5051 and the Missouri-style bills target. (Caution: a syndicated claim that "OpenAI models breached Hugging Face — 15,000 tokens, 72 hours" is fabricated; disregard.)
